                 ######transformers architecture####

this is literary the architecture behind GPT and other LLMs.
-google enginners released the paper "Attention is all you need" in 2017 which led to this architecture.
we used to work with traditional statistical model, 

Transformers process sequences like sentences using attention instead of recurrence, allowing them to see all words at once and decide which ones matter most.
Each layer combines multi-head self-attention to find relationships between words and feed-forward networks to process information further.
By stacking these layers, transformers learn deep patterns in data, forming the foundation of models like GPT
